The MyDP Tutorial

Welcome to MyDP.

The digital home of MUNDP 2027. This short guide walks you through the portal in under a minute.

A Paperless Conference

The conference, digitized.

MUNDP now runs on screens instead of paper. Resolutions, amendments, notes and voting all happen here, live, on any device you bring.

Live resolutions Digital voting Instant notes

Resolutions

Every clause, on the record.

The Resolutions tab lists every document before the house. Open one to read it live, follow amendments as they are proposed, and watch its status move from submitted to debated.

Your Part

{% if current_user and current_user.role == 'chair' %}

Run the chamber.

Approve submissions, mark documents as In Debate, keep the tally and open the vote. My Committee is your chair's desk.

{% elif current_user and current_user.role == 'advisor' %}

Follow your delegations.

The Tally tab tracks every point your delegations earn, updated live as chairs record speeches, points of information and amendments.

{% elif current_user and current_user.role in ('exec_gc', 'dsg', 'admin') %}

Oversee every chamber.

Browse the committees, follow the calendar and keep the conference in order. Every chamber reports here, as it happens.

{% else %}

Submit, debate, vote.

When your chair grants you a submission window, upload your clause from the Submission tab. When voting opens on a resolution, cast your vote right on its page.

{% endif %}
{% if current_user and current_user.role in ('exec_gc', 'dsg') %}

International Summit

The floor, assembled.

Each committee sends one resolution forward. The Summit tab collects them all, chamber by chamber, as the conference builds toward its final session.

{% else %}

Note Passing

Notes, without the paper.

Send a note to the Chairboard or another delegation from the Notes tab. Delivery is instant, and chairs review every note that passes.

{% endif %}
